Create a polished professional 3D game environment prop concept sheet for FIVE distinctly different low flat-topped stepping rocks for the game Between Two Poles. This replaces a rejected overly geometric, ugly octagonal platform concept. Make these look like appealing naturally weathered rocks designed by an experienced stylized environment artist, NOT cylinders, stacked pancakes, machined platforms, perfect polygons, concrete blocks, or staircases.

Style: charming understated low-poly 3D game art with crisp irregular planar facets, deliberately asymmetrical silhouettes, broad readable color planes, restrained hand-painted shading. Similar fidelity to a small stylized planetary puzzle game with red/blue bar magnets, icy light-blue tiles and a blocky astronaut. Clean orthographic three-quarter views. Attractive sophisticated prop design; no photographic noisy stone texture. Muted blue-gray and slightly warm gray stone, softly illuminated edges and grounded ambient occlusion. Pale warm neutral studio background. No plants, snow, ice spikes or other decorations: evaluate the bare rock forms first.

Layout: landscape concept board, three evenly spaced designs across the upper row, two centered on the lower row. Exactly five numbered designs, each shown as a large hero three-quarter view plus a small accurate top view beneath it. Generous negative space and equal scale. Discrete number labels only: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05. A small clean title at top: LOW ROCK / SHAPE EXPLORATION. Small subtitle: H = 0.55. No other text.

Mandatory physical constraint for ALL FIVE: same total height 0.55 world units with width roughly 1.15–1.35 world units fitting within a 1.5-unit square tile. LOW squat rocks. All have a generous continuous nearly level upper landing surface covering at least 65 percent of footprint; a small humanoid can land and stand there after being thrown from a portal. The tops must feel naturally worn and flat, not domed, spiky, broken into disconnected chunks or deeply cracked. Keep interesting facets and chips mostly on sidewalls, bevels and perimeter. Top surface uninterrupted at the center.

01: Broad asymmetrical rounded-triangular glacial boulder, one long sloping beveled flank and one short blunt upright flank; small chipped corner; expansive worn flat summit. Strong pleasing distinctive silhouette.
02: A compact elongated irregular slab with a shallow diagonal natural mineral seam confined to one sidewall, one undercut chipped corner, and an unbroken flat cap. No deep split across the standing area.
03: Squat softly rounded river stone interpreted in broad low-poly facets, a slightly kidney-shaped asymmetric perimeter, smooth wide flat top; a subtle scooped side recess away from the top center. More organic than the angular designs.
04: A rugged low wedge boulder with a wide level plateau; one shoulder protrudes lower down like a natural stone buttress, clustered planar cuts down the sides, a chipped nose. One coherent solid rock, not a pile of rocks or a staircase.
05: A broad irregular flat cap over an eroded narrow-waisted lower body, subtle diagonal strata only on side surfaces, gently broken perimeter. Not circular, not cylindrical, not evenly tiered. Flat wide landing area; a convincing single natural low boulder.

Ensure all five feel related in art direction but immediately distinct in outline and sidewall treatment. Their exact flat tops should be clear in the paired top views. Beautiful presentation for a user to choose two props. No characters, magnets, scenery or extra unnumbered variations.
